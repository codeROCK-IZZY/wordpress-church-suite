Volunteer rotas that fill every place without overloading anyone, people and households, giving records with year-end statements, and sermons with a podcast feed.

== Description ==

* Rotas planned in one click: only trained roles, never on dates away, one role per service, within each person's monthly limit, shared fairly, avoiding back-to-back Sundays and keeping households together when they ask.
* "Can you serve?" emails with Accept and Decline links that work without logging in. A decline asks the best other person straight away.
* People with roles, how often they serve, dates away and households.
* Giving records in whole cents, refunds kept on record, and printable year-end statements by fund.
* Sermons with speakers, series and scripture, an archive shortcode and a podcast feed at /feed/sermons/.
* Personal data export and erasure (gift amounts are kept anonymously for accounting).

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin folder to /wp-content/plugins/ and activate it.
2. Set service times and the roles each service needs under Congregation → Settings.
3. Add people under Congregation → People, then plan and send the rota under Congregation → Rota.
