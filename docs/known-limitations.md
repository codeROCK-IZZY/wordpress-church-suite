# Known limitations

- **The rota is not proven optimal.** At least 9% of places went unfilled under every method that keeps the rules. That
  points to real scarcity, but the gap to the best possible rota was not measured.
- **The evaluation churches are simulated.** Roles, availability, household wishes and decline rates are assumptions,
  and the weights should be checked against a real church's history.
- **One role per service per person.** Someone who plays in the band *and* runs slides at a small church is
  scheduled for one of them.
- **No events or check-in.** There are no children's check-in labels, small-group rosters or event sign-ups.
- **No online giving.** Gifts are recorded, not taken (ADR 3). Statements are printable HTML, not PDFs or emails.
- **Households are numbers**, not linked family records with relationships.
- **Answer links do not expire on a timer** (ADR 2), only when the place changes hands or the date passes.
- **No reminders before a Sunday**, and no swap requests between volunteers.
- **The podcast feed reads audio sizes you enter** (or from the media library). It does not fetch remote files to
  measure them.
